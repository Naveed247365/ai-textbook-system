# Perception for Human-Robot Interaction - Advanced

## Mathematical Foundations

### Probabilistic Framework for HRI Perception

The HRI perception problem can be formulated as a Bayesian inference problem:
**P(H_t | O_{1:t}) ∝ P(O_t | H_t) × ∫ P(H_t | H_{t-1}) × P(H_{t-1} | O_{1:t-1}) dH_{t-1}**

Where H_t represents the human state (pose, expression, intention) and O_t represents observations from multiple modalities.

### Information-Theoretic Measures

The mutual information between human states and robot actions quantifies interaction quality:
**I(H;A) = ∫∫ P(h,a) × log[P(h,a)/(P(h)×P(a))] dh da**

The Kullback-Leibler divergence measures the difference between expected and actual human states:
**D_{KL}(P||Q) = ∫ P(h) × log[P(h)/Q(h)] dh**

### Cramér-Rao Lower Bound for Human State Estimation

The fundamental limit on human state estimation accuracy:
**Cov(θ̂) ≥ I(θ)^{-1}**

Where I(θ) is the Fisher Information Matrix for the human state vector θ.

## Advanced Human Detection and Tracking

### Multi-Modal Human Detection with Uncertainty Quantification

The detection confidence with uncertainty:
**P(person | x) = σ(W^T × φ(x) + b) ± σ_uncertainty**

Where σ is the sigmoid function, φ(x) are features, and σ_uncertainty quantifies detection uncertainty.

### Deep Multi-Object Tracking with Appearance Modeling

The association probability with deep appearance features:
**P(track_i, detection_j) = P_motion × P_appearance × P_social**

Where:
- P_motion = exp(-||d_pred - d_j||²_Σ⁻¹) / √|2πΣ|
- P_appearance = exp(-||f_track_i - f_j||²)
- P_social = exp(-α × social_cost)

### 3D Human Pose Estimation with Temporal Consistency

The temporal pose optimization:
**min_{R,T} Σ_t Σ_i w_{t,i} × ||π(R_t, T_t, X_{t,i}) - x_{t,i}||² + λ × Σ_t ||R_t - R_{t-1}||²**

Where R_t, T_t are rotation and translation at time t, X_{t,i} is the 3D joint position, and x_{t,i} is the 2D projection.

### Graph-Based Human Pose Estimation

The pose estimation as a graph optimization problem:
**min_θ Σ_e ψ_e(θ_i, θ_j) + Σ_v ψ_v(θ_i)**

Where ψ_e represents edge potentials between joints and ψ_v represents unary potentials for individual joints.

## Advanced Facial Recognition and Expression Analysis

### Deep Face Recognition with Metric Learning

The embedding-based recognition:
**d(f_i, f_j) = ||f_i - f_j||₂**

Where f_i, f_j are face embeddings from a deep neural network trained with triplet loss:
**L_triplet = max(0, ||f_a - f_p||₂² - ||f_a - f_n||₂² + α)**

Where f_a is anchor, f_p is positive, f_n is negative, and α is margin.

### Facial Action Unit Detection with Graph Neural Networks

The AU detection using graph convolutions:
**h_i^{(l+1)} = σ(W^{(l)} × Σ_{j∈N(i)} (A_{ij} × h_j^{(l)}) + b^{(l)})**

Where h_i is the feature vector for facial landmark i, A is the adjacency matrix, and N(i) represents neighbors of landmark i.

### Emotion Recognition with Attention Mechanisms

The attention-weighted emotion classification:
**e = softmax(W_e × Σ_i α_i × h_i)**

Where α_i = exp(e_i) / Σ_j exp(e_j) and e_i = v^T × tanh(W × [h; h_i]).

## Advanced Gesture Recognition

### Temporal Gesture Recognition with LSTM Networks

The sequence modeling for dynamic gestures:
**h_t = LSTM(x_t, h_{t-1})**
**P(gesture | sequence) = softmax(W_g × h_T)**

Where h_T is the final hidden state after processing the entire sequence.

### 3D Hand Pose Estimation with Volumetric Representations

The hand pose estimation in 3D:
**V(x,y,z) = Σ_i w_i × K(x - x_i, y - y_i, z - z_i)**

Where V is the volumetric representation and K is a 3D kernel function.

### Continuous Gesture Recognition with Hidden Markov Models

The continuous recognition model:
**P(O | λ) = Σ_{S} P(O | S, λ) × P(S | λ)**

Where O is the observation sequence, S represents state sequences, and λ represents HMM parameters.

## Advanced Audio Processing for HRI

### Multi-Channel Speech Enhancement

The beamforming optimization:
**w_{opt} = argmin_w w^H × Φ_{nn} × w subject to w^H × d = 1**

Where w is the beamforming vector, Φ_{nn} is the noise covariance matrix, and d is the desired response vector.

### Speaker Diarization with Neural Networks

The speaker assignment problem:
**z_t = argmax_k P(speaker_k | x_t, Z_{1:t-1})**

Where z_t indicates the speaker at time t and x_t are acoustic features.

### Audio-Visual Fusion with Deep Learning

The multimodal fusion network:
**f_{fused} = W_f × [f_visual; f_audio] + b_f**

Where f_visual and f_audio are features from respective modalities.

## Social Signal Processing

### Proxemics Modeling with Gaussian Processes

The personal space modeling:
**s(x) ~ GP(μ(x), k(x, x'))**

Where s(x) represents the social comfort level at position x, μ(x) is the mean function, and k(x, x') is the kernel function.

### Gaze Estimation with Geometric Constraints

The 3D gaze estimation:
**g = R × (g_2D - c) + t**

Where g is the 3D gaze direction, R is rotation matrix, g_2D is 2D gaze, c is camera center, and t is translation.

### Social Engagement Recognition with Graph Convolutional Networks

The engagement prediction:
**E = σ(W_e × GCN(A, X))**

Where A is the adjacency matrix of social interactions, X is node features, and GCN is the graph convolutional network.

## Advanced Multi-Modal Fusion

### Bayesian Fusion with Model Uncertainty

The uncertainty-aware fusion:
**P(state | observations) = Σ_i P(state | obs_i, model_i) × P(model_i | observations)**

### Deep Multi-Modal Learning with Cross-Modal Attention

The cross-attention mechanism:
**A = softmax(Q × K^T / √d_k)**
**V_attended = A × V**

Where Q, K, V are query, key, and value matrices from different modalities.

### Variational Autoencoders for Multi-Modal Representation Learning

The multi-modal VAE:
**L = E_{q(z|x,y)}[log p(x,y|z)] - KL(q(z|x,y) || p(z))**

Where x, y are modalities and z is the shared latent representation.

## Privacy-Preserving HRI Perception

### Differential Privacy for Face Recognition

The differentially private mechanism:
**M(D) = f(D) + Noise(λ)**

Where λ = Δf / ε and Δf is the sensitivity of function f.

### Homomorphic Encryption for Biometric Processing

The encrypted computation:
**E[f(x)] = f(E[x])**

Where E[x] represents the encrypted form of x.

### Federated Learning for HRI Perception

The federated optimization:
**θ* = argmin_θ Σ_k λ_k × F_k(θ)**

Where F_k(θ) is the local objective function and λ_k are weights.

## Real-Time Optimization

### Model Compression for Edge Deployment

**L_{compressed} = L_{original} + α × L_{distill} + β × L_{prune}**

Where L_{distill} is knowledge distillation loss and L_{prune} is sparsity regularization.

### Neural Architecture Search for HRI Perception

The architecture optimization:
**(θ*, A*) = argmin_{θ,A} L(θ, A) + C(A)**

Where A represents the architecture and C(A) is the computational cost constraint.

## Advanced Evaluation Metrics

### Social Interaction Quality Metrics

**Engagement Index**: EI = Σ_t I(attentive_t) / T
**Social Comfort Score**: SCS = (1/T) × Σ_t comfort_t
**Naturalness Score**: NS = (1/N) × Σ_i naturalness_i

### Fairness and Bias Metrics

**Demographic Parity**: P(Ŷ = 1 | A = 0) = P(Ŷ = 1 | A = 1)
**Equal Opportunity**: P(Ŷ = 1 | Y = 1, A = 0) = P(Ŷ = 1 | Y = 1, A = 1)
**Individual Fairness**: |P(Ŷ = 1 | x_i) - P(Ŷ = 1 | x_j)| ≤ δ × d(x_i, x_j)

Where A represents protected attributes and d is a distance metric.

## Robustness and Adversarial Considerations

### Adversarial Training for HRI Perception

The adversarial loss:
**L_{adv} = L_{clean} + λ × L_{perturbed}**

Where L_{perturbed} = L(θ, x + δ, y) and δ is the adversarial perturbation.

### Domain Adaptation for Cross-Cultural HRI

The domain adaptation loss:
**L_{total} = L_{source} + λ × L_{domain} + β × L_{adversarial}**

## Active Perception in HRI

### Information-Theoretic Active Perception

The optimal action selection:
**a* = argmax_a I(H; O_a | past_observations)**

Where I is mutual information between human state H and observation O_a under action a.

### Exploration-Exploitation in HRI Learning

The multi-armed bandit formulation:
**a* = argmax_a (Q(a) + c × √(log(t) / N_a))**

Where Q(a) is the expected reward, N_a is the number of times action a was taken, and c is exploration parameter.

## Learning from Demonstration in HRI

### Imitation Learning for Social Behaviors

The behavioral cloning objective:
**θ* = argmin_θ E_{(s,a)~π_demo} [||π_θ(s) - a||²]**

### Inverse Reinforcement Learning for Social Preferences

The reward learning:
**R* = argmax_R E_{π_expert}[R] - E_{π_random}[R]**
