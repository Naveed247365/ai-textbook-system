# Key Concepts

## Perception
The ability of robots to interpret sensory data from their environment using sensors like cameras, lidar, and touch sensors.

## Planning
The process of determining a sequence of actions to achieve a goal, taking into account environmental constraints and robot capabilities.

## Control
The execution of planned actions through precise motor control and feedback systems.

## Learning
The ability to improve performance through experience, including supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning.

## Adaptation
The capacity to adjust behavior based on changing environmental conditions or performance feedback.
