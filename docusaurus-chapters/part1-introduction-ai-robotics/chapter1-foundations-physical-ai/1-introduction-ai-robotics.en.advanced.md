# Foundations of Physical AI & Robotics (English Advanced Version)

Welcome to Chapter 1 of our AI & Robotics textbook. This chapter provides a comprehensive exploration of the fundamental concepts underlying Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics, with emphasis on rigorous technical understanding.

## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, we delve deeply into:

- [What is Physical AI?](./1.1-what-is-physical-ai.en.advanced) - Rigorous examination of Physical AI concepts, differentiating from traditional AI with mathematical foundations
- [Humanoid Robotics](./1.2-humanoid-robotics.en.advanced) - Comprehensive analysis of humanoid robot design, kinematics, dynamics, and control theory
- [Historical Context](./1.3-historical-context.en.advanced) - Detailed examination of the evolution of AI and robotics with technical milestones
- [Current Applications](./1.4-current-applications.en.advanced) - Advanced analysis of state-of-the-art implementations with technical details
- [Future Prospects](./1.5-future-prospects.en.advanced) - In-depth exploration of emerging technical paradigms and ethical frameworks
- [Key Concepts](./1.6-key-concepts.en.advanced) - Mathematical foundations and technical specifications for core concepts

This chapter establishes the rigorous technical foundation required for advanced understanding of the subject matter and prepares the reader for complex implementations.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

1. Precisely define Physical AI with mathematical rigor and differentiate from traditional AI approaches
2. Analyze humanoid robot design with understanding of kinematic and dynamic models
3. Critically evaluate the technical evolution of AI and robotics with focus on computational advances
4. Evaluate current industry implementations with technical analysis
5. Assess future trends with consideration of technical feasibility and ethical implications
6. Master key concepts with mathematical understanding of perception, planning, control, and adaptation algorithms
