# Foundations of Physical AI & Robotics

Welcome to Chapter 1 of our Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics textbook. This chapter introduces you to the fundamental concepts that underpin the entire field of Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics.

## Chapter Overview

In this chapter, we'll explore:

- [What is Physical AI?](./1.1-what-is-physical-ai) - Understanding the core concept of Physical AI and how it differs from traditional AI
- [Humanoid Robotics](./1.2-humanoid-robotics) - Exploring the design and purpose of humanoid robots
- [Historical Context](./1.3-historical-context) - The evolution of AI and robotics from early developments to today
- [Current Applications](./1.4-current-applications) - How AI and robotics are being used today across various industries
- [Future Prospects](./1.5-future-prospects) - The emerging trends and ethical considerations
- [Key Concepts](./1.6-key-concepts) - Fundamental concepts every practitioner should understand

This chapter provides the essential foundation for all subsequent chapters in this textbook. Understanding these principles is crucial as we dive deeper into the specific technologies and applications in the coming chapters.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

1. Define Physical AI and distinguish it from traditional AI approaches
2. Explain the advantages and challenges of humanoid robot design
3. Describe the historical progression of AI and robotics
4. Identify current applications across different industries
5. Discuss future prospects and ethical considerations
6. Understand key concepts such as perception, planning, control, and adaptation
