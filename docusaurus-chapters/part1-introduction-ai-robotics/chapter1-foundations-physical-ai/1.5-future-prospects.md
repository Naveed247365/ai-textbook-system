# Future Prospects

## Humanoid Robots
Research continues toward developing humanoid robots that can interact naturally with humans and perform complex tasks in human environments.

## Swarm Robotics
This emerging field explores the coordination of large groups of simple robots to perform complex tasks collectively.

## Ethical AI & Robotics
As robots become more autonomous and integrated into society, the need for ethical frameworks and regulations becomes more pressing.
