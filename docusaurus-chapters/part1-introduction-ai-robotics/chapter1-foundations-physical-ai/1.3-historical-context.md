# Historical Context

## Early Development (1940s-1960s)
The field of robotics began with the first industrial robots in the 1960s. Meanwhile, AI research began in the 1950s with foundational work by Alan Turing and others, asking fundamental questions about machine intelligence.

## The Emergence of AI & Robotics (1970s-1980s)
During this period, researchers began developing the theoretical foundations for combining AI and robotics. Early mobile robots like Shakey the Robot (1966-1972) demonstrated the potential for intelligent, autonomous machines.

## The Modern Era (1990s-2000s)
This era saw the development of more sophisticated robots with improved sensors, actuators, and control systems. The introduction of robot operating systems (ROS) began to standardize robot development.

## The Renaissance (2010s-Present)
The current era is characterized by advances in machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision, which have dramatically improved robots' ability to perceive and interact with their environments. AI techniques now enable robots to learn from experience, adapt to new situations, and even collaborate with humans.
