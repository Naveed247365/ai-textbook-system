# What is Physical AI?

Physical AI represents the integration of artificial intelligence with the physical world, focusing on systems that can perceive, reason, and act in physical environments. Unlike traditional AI that operates primarily in virtual spaces (like chatbots or recommendation systems), Physical AI encompasses systems that must navigate the complexities of the physical world including physics, materials, and real-time interactions.

## Key Characteristics of Physical AI
- **Embodiment**: Having a physical form that interacts with the environment
- **Real-time processing**: Making decisions under temporal constraints
- **Physics awareness**: Understanding and working within physical laws
- **Sensory integration**: Combining multiple sensory inputs to understand the environment
- **Action execution**: Performing physical tasks that change the environment

## Distinction from Traditional AI
Traditional AI often operates on symbolic or digital representations, while Physical AI must handle the uncertainty, variability, and complexity of real-world physics. Physical AI systems must be robust to noise, adaptable to changing environments, and capable of safe interaction with humans and the environment.

## Applications of Physical AI
- Autonomous vehicles navigating complex traffic scenarios
- Robotic manipulation in unstructured environments
- Collaborative robots working alongside humans
- Autonomous drones for surveillance and delivery
- Medical robots for surgery and patient care
