# Current Applications

## Industrial Robotics
Industrial robots have transformed manufacturing, performing tasks such as welding, painting, assembly, and material handling with high precision and repeatability.

## Service Robotics
Service robots assist humans in various environments, including healthcare (surgical robots), hospitality (room service robots), and domestic settings (vacuum robots).

## Autonomous Vehicles
Self-driving cars, drones, and other autonomous vehicles rely on AI algorithms for perception, navigation, and decision-making in complex, dynamic environments.

## Healthcare Robotics
Medical robots assist in surgery, rehabilitation, patient care, and drug discovery, often performing tasks with greater precision than is possible manually.

## Agricultural Robotics
Robots are being deployed in agriculture for crop monitoring, harvesting, and precision farming, helping to increase efficiency and reduce environmental impact.
